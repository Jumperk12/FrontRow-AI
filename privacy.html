Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026 • Effective Immediately

Summary in Plain English: FrontRow AI is built with privacy at its core. Video rendering and enhancements occur locally on your Apple device using Apple CoreML and Metal shaders. We do not sell your personal data, we do not track you across other apps, and extracted audio files are processed in secure ephemeral memory and purged immediately.

1 Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs how FrontRow AI ("we", "us", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users ("User", "you") of the FrontRow AI mobile application available on the Apple App Store.

We are committed to protecting your privacy in full compliance with Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable international privacy frameworks.

2 Information We Collect & How It Is Handled

2.1 Account & Authentication Information

When you register or sign in to FrontRow AI via email, your authentication is handled securely through Supabase. We store only your email address and an encrypted user identifier to manage your account status, preset preferences, and subscription entitlements. We never store plaintext passwords.

2.2 Video & Photographic Media (On-Device Processing)

To enhance your concert recordings, FrontRow AI requests read and write access to your iOS Photos library via Apple's standard PHPhotoLibrary permissions.

  • Local Video Processing: All video enhancements — including Strobe De-Flicker, Stage Smoke Piercing, Laser Contrast, and 4K HDR Color Grading — are executed 100% locally on your iPhone hardware using Apple CoreML neural engines and Metal GPU shaders.
  • No Video Uploads: Your video frames, faces, and visuals never leave your device. They are never transmitted to our servers or any third-party clouds.

2.3 Audio Media for AI Crowd-Noise Cancellation

When you enable the AI Crowd Noise Cancellation (Neural Analog DJ Decrowd) feature:

  • Only the extracted audio track (not video frames) is temporarily sent via encrypted HTTPS/TLS 1.3 to our specialized high-performance audio restoration engine (Neural Analog API).
  • Ephemeral Processing: Audio files are held exclusively in volatile memory on dedicated GPU clusters solely for the duration required to isolate music and suppress crowd screams.
  • Zero Model Training: Your audio is never used to train, fine-tune, or retrain machine learning models.
  • Automatic Purging: Processed audio tracks are permanently deleted immediately upon download completion, or purged automatically within one (1) hour.

2.4 Financial & Payment Data

FrontRow AI uses Apple StoreKit 2 for all in-app purchases, weekly passes, and monthly subscriptions. We do not collect, view, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials. All transactions are handled directly and securely by Apple Inc.

2.5 Analytics & Telemetry

We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers, cross-site trackers, or IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers) tracking. We may receive non-identifiable, aggregated technical diagnostics (such as crash stack traces and app version counts) through Apple's opt-in TestFlight and App Store Connect crash reports.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the limited data we collect exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and sync your saved concert enhancement presets.
  • To verify your subscription tier or 3-day free trial entitlement.
  • To perform crowd shouting reduction and audio restoration requested by you.
  • To prevent fraudulent trial abuse via anonymous cryptographic hardware Keychain hashes.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

4 We Never Sell Your Data

FrontRow AI does not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information, email, videos, or audio to data brokers, advertisers, or third parties under any circumstances.

5 Third-Party Service Providers

We work only with vetted infrastructure partners who adhere to rigorous security standards:

  • Supabase Inc. — Secure user authentication and database management (encrypted at rest and in transit).
  • Neural Analog — Cloud GPU audio processing API (ephemeral audio isolation, zero storage).
  • Apple Inc. — iOS operating system, StoreKit 2 in-app purchases, and Apple Keychain.

6 Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account email only as long as you maintain an active account with us. You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the app under Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing shopifydotstore@gmail.com. Upon request, your record is purged permanently within 30 days.

7 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate or incomplete account details.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the total deletion of your personal account data.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you, alter your prices, or diminish service quality for exercising your privacy rights.

8 Children's Privacy

FrontRow AI is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

9 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after modifications constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Content Consent & Anti-NSFW Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026 • Strict Zero-Tolerance Standard

Zero-Tolerance NSFW Policy: FrontRow AI is engineered exclusively for live concert, music festival, and DJ stage performances. The uploading, processing, enhancing, or exporting of sexually explicit media, nudity, violent content, or non-consensual recordings is strictly prohibited and results in immediate, permanent account termination.

1 Purpose & Core Mission

FrontRow AI was created to solve a singular, creative problem: helping concertgoers, DJs, and music enthusiasts transform dark, strobe-flickering, crowd-drowned concert recordings into pristine 4K HDR live performance masters.

To maintain an ethical, safe, and legal creative ecosystem, all users must adhere strictly to this Content Consent & Anti-NSFW Policy. By accessing or using FrontRow AI, you explicitly affirm that you will only process content for which you possess proper consent and that strictly complies with these guidelines.

2 Mandatory Content Consent & Copyright Authorization

2.1 Ownership & Permission Warranties

Before uploading, importing, or processing any video or audio file in FrontRow AI, you represent and warrant that:

  • Lawful Capture: You are the author/recorder of the media, or you have obtained explicit written authorization, license, or consent from the copyright owner to record and enhance the footage.
  • Venue & Event Rules: You captured the recording in compliance with all applicable event ticketing terms, venue regulations, and artist photography/videography guidelines.
  • Third-Party Privacy Rights: Your recording does not infringe upon the reasonable expectation of privacy of any individual depicted in the footage.

2.2 Strict Prohibition Against Non-Consensual Recording

You are strictly prohibited from uploading or processing:

  • Any surreptitious, hidden-camera, or voyeuristic recordings made without the knowledge and consent of the individuals recorded.
  • Footage captured in private, restricted, or confidential areas (including backstage dressing rooms, private VIP suites, restrooms, or locker facilities).
  • Any media intended to harass, dox, intimidate, humiliate, or stalk any artist, venue employee, or fellow attendee.

3 Zero-Tolerance Anti-NSFW Policy

FrontRow AI maintains an absolute, zero-tolerance policy against the processing or dissemination of Not Safe For Work (NSFW) material.

3.1 Explicitly Banned Media Categories

You agree NEVER to import, process, enhance, decrowd, or export:

  • Sexually Explicit Material & Nudity: Any depiction of sexual acts, intercourse, masturbation, exposed genitalia, buttocks, or female breasts, regardless of artistic intent.
  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any imagery, recording, or audio involving the sexualization, abuse, or exploitation of minors. Violations of this nature will trigger immediate account deletion and mandatory referral to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and federal law enforcement agencies.
  • Graphic Violence & Physical Harm: Footage depicting physical assaults, fights, stabbings, severe bodily injury, torture, self-harm, suicides, or animal cruelty.
  • Hate Speech & Harassment: Content promoting discrimination, dehumanization, threats of violence, or racial/ethnic slurs against any group or individual.
  • Illicit Substance Abuse & Weapon Violence: Recordings glorifying or demonstrating the manufacturing of illegal drugs, weapons trafficking, or terrorist activities.

4 Algorithmic Screening & Enforcement Actions

To enforce this policy and ensure user safety across our platform:

  • Automated Pre-Processing Scans: Our pipeline utilizes automated perceptual hashing and content moderation safety filters to detect prohibited NSFW content before cloud GPU audio allocation.
  • Immediate Processing Refusal: If media is flagged as containing prohibited elements, the enhancement job is immediately aborted, and temporary audio files are purged on the spot.
  • Permanent Account Revocation: Accounts found in violation will be permanently banned without notice. Hardware identifiers associated with trial abuse or NSFW distribution will be blacklisted.
  • No Refund for Policy Violations: Users terminated for violating our Anti-NSFW or Consent policies forfeit all subscription rights and are not eligible for any refund.

5 User Indemnification & Legal Liability

You agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless FrontRow AI, its developers, operators, and cloud infrastructure partners from and against any claims, liabilities, damages, losses, or legal expenses (including reasonable attorney fees) arising from:

  • Any video or audio media uploaded or processed by you that infringes third-party copyrights, trademarks, or privacy rights.
  • Your failure to obtain necessary recording consent from artists, attendees, or venue operators.
  • Any violation by you of this Content Consent & Anti-NSFW Policy.

6 Copyright Holders & DMCA Takedown Procedure

We respect the intellectual property of performers, record labels, and content creators. Because FrontRow AI processes videos locally on user devices and purges cloud audio files upon download, FrontRow AI does not host a public media gallery or video streaming network.

However, if you are a copyright owner or an authorized representative and believe that any promotional asset, tutorial, or service element infringes upon your copyright, please submit a formal DMCA Notice containing:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  2. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing and its location.
  3. Your contact information (name, address, phone number, and email).
  4. A statement of good faith belief that the disputed use is unauthorized.
  5. A statement under penalty of perjury that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.

Send DMCA notices directly to our designated copyright agent at shopifydotstore@gmail.com.
